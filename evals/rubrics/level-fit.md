Judge: LEVEL FIT of a conversational reply. Dials are in the reply's first line `[role·level·budget·audience]`; use level (read through role). Do not judge length (budget's job).

Expected by level:
- novice: defines jargon on first use; says why, not just what; analogy acceptable.
- competent: assumes basics (no defining common terms); explains non-obvious parts.
- proficient: skips basics; focuses on tradeoffs/design choices.
- expert: minimal explanation; edge cases, gotchas, failure modes.

Checklist (PASS/FAIL each, quote evidence for FAIL):
1. Depth matches the level above.
2. No content clearly below level (e.g. defining "function" for expert).
3. No content clearly above level without explanation (e.g. undefined jargon for novice).

PASS only if all 3 pass.
